# Errata & corrections policy

Public debate runs on trust, and trust runs on correcting errors loudly.

## Policy

1. **Anyone may file a correction** — use the
   [correction issue template](https://github.com/CristianNichifor/digital-romania-atlas/issues/new?template=correction.yml),
   or email the author (see [AUTHORS.md](AUTHORS.md)).
2. **Every accepted correction is logged below**, with the date, what changed
   and who spotted it (with their permission). Corrections are never silently
   folded into the data.
3. **Factual corrections to "current" claims** (i.e. claims sourced from MAI
   documents or EU regulations) are fixed as fast as possible — typically days,
   not weeks.
4. **Disagreements about "proposed" content** are design opinions, not factual
   claims; they are not "errors". File a discussion instead — the steelman
   section in the Analiză view is where strong counter-arguments belong.
5. The changelog notes every content correction so citations can be traced
   across versions.
